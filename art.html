about me ｡ .⋆ ˚✩₊.

Hello there! My name is May and I am a filipino-american artist! I am self-taught and I primarily create digital art. I got really into art when I was like 9, and I eventually started sharing my art online in 2020. Even though my art sucked, I enjoyed interacting with fellow artists online! Seeing other people's art inspired me to expand and improve my own art skills. I primarily draw fanart of media that I am currently obsessed with :p. Art is mainly a side-hobby for me, but it is still something I am very passionate about.

terms of service ⋆˙⟡₊

Just because my art is online, does not mean that you have permission to use for anything, so here are some rules I have.

  • DO NOT use my art in any AI models or use it for NFT/crypto use.
  • DO NOT repost my art (as in downloading my art and posting it yourself as a new post on an online platform). Even if you add credits, I still do not want you reposting my art. (Resharing my art through implmented features on social media, like tumblr reblogs or instagram stories/repost, are okay).
  • Do not trace/draw over my art or try to make it your own.
  • Do not alter my art (color filters are ok though).
  • Ask if you want to use my art for your personal projects, such as video edits.
  • Using my art as pfp or banners is okay as long as I am credited, but please ask first.

If you are unsure of anything, please don't be afraid to contact me and ask!
